How to use this calendar

Welcome to your 30-day social media content calendar. This calendar gives you a full month of ready-made post ideas designed specifically for hair salons, beauty salons, barbers, and beauty therapists.

1

Start on any day. The days are numbered 1 to 30. Begin whenever works for you.

2

Customise the captions. Add your salon name, stylist names, product brands, and specific services to make posts feel personal and authentic.

3

Take before and after photos. These are your most powerful content. Good lighting, clean backgrounds, and consistent angles make all the difference.

4

Film quick reels and stories. Short video clips of you working are incredibly engaging. Your phone is all the equipment you need.

5

Post consistently. The salons that post regularly are the ones that stay fully booked. Aim for at least 4 to 5 posts per week.
