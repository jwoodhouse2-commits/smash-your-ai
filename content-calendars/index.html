What format are the calendars in?

Each calendar is delivered as a beautifully designed HTML file that you can view on any device. You can also print it to PDF directly from your browser (File > Print > Save as PDF) for a clean, printable version. The design is optimised for both digital and print use.

Can I use the same calendar more than once?

Absolutely. The post ideas are designed to be evergreen and reusable. You can cycle through them month after month, customising the captions each time to keep things fresh. One purchase gives you content ideas you can use indefinitely.

What if my industry is not listed?

We are adding new industries regularly. In the meantime, many of the post ideas (behind-the-scenes content, testimonials, engagement questions, tips) work across any business. The bundle is a great option if you want to mix and match ideas from multiple industries.

Do I need to post every single day?

Not at all. The calendar gives you 30 ideas, but you can pick and choose the ones that work best for you. Posting 3 to 5 times per week is more than enough for most small businesses. Use the calendar as a menu, not a strict schedule.
