How to use this calendar

Welcome to your 30-day social media content calendar. This calendar gives you a full month of ready-made post ideas designed specifically for accountants, bookkeepers, and financial advisors.

1

Start on any day. The days are numbered 1 to 30 so you can begin whenever suits you.

2

Customise the captions. Add your own firm name, specialisms, local details, and specific tax figures to make each post feel personal and authoritative.

3

Follow the platform suggestions or repurpose each post across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook. LinkedIn tends to work especially well for accountants.

4

Create simple visuals. Use Canva templates or AI image tools to create professional graphics quickly. A clean branded template goes a long way.

5

Batch and schedule. Set aside time each week to create and schedule posts using Buffer, Hootsuite, or LinkedIn's built-in scheduler.
