How to use this calendar

Welcome to your 30-day social media content calendar. This calendar gives you a month of ready-made post ideas specifically designed for restaurants, cafes, and food businesses.

1

Start on any day. The calendar is numbered Day 1 to Day 30 so you can begin whenever you like. There is no need to wait for the first of the month.

2

Use the captions as they are or customise them. Each post includes a suggested caption. Feel free to tweak the wording, add your own dish names, or include local details to make them feel more personal.

3

Follow the platform suggestions. Each post recommends a platform (Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn), but you can repurpose any post across multiple channels.

4

Create the visuals. Each post suggests a content type (photo, reel, carousel, or text). Use your phone, Canva, or AI image tools to create the visuals quickly.

5

Schedule in advance. Batch-create your content and schedule it using tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, or Meta Business Suite for a stress-free month.
