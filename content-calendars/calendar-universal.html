How to use this calendar

Welcome to your universal 30-day social media content calendar. This template works for any business in any industry. Every post includes a fill-in-the-blanks caption with [BRACKETS] where you add your own details. No guessing, no staring at a blank screen.

1

Start on any day. The calendar is numbered Day 1 to Day 30 so you can begin whenever you like. There is no need to wait for the first of the month.

2

Fill in the [BRACKETS]. Each caption has placeholders like [YOUR BUSINESS], [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE], and [YOUR AREA]. Replace them with your own details to make each post personal and specific to your business.

3

Follow the platform suggestions. Each post recommends a platform (LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook), but you can repurpose any post across multiple channels.

4

Use the hashtag guidance. Instead of specific hashtags, each post tells you the types of hashtags to use (e.g. industry, local, trending). Pick ones that fit your niche.

5

Reuse every month. This calendar is designed to repeat. The content themes rotate through education, engagement, promotion, and storytelling, so your feed stays varied even if you run it again and again.
