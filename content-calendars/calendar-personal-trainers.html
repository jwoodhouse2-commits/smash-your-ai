How to use this calendar

Welcome to your 30-day social media content calendar. This calendar gives you a full month of ready-made post ideas designed specifically for personal trainers and fitness professionals.

1

Start on any day. The days are numbered 1 to 30 so you can begin whenever suits you.

2

Customise the captions. Add your own personality, client names (with permission), specific exercises, or local gym details.

3

Follow the platform suggestions or repurpose each post across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

4

Film short clips. Many posts suggest video content. Your phone camera is all you need. Authenticity beats production quality every time.

5

Batch and schedule. Set aside an hour each week to create and schedule posts using tools like Later, Buffer, or Meta Business Suite.
