Brainstorming is the thing AI is genuinely, almost-cheating-level good at. Every time you're stuck, you can produce 20 ideas in 30 seconds and pick the three that aren't terrible.

## Why AI brainstorms better than you

Not because it's more creative. Because it's **less attached**.

When you brainstorm alone, you filter as you go. You dismiss ideas before they leave your head. You gravitate to the safe middle.

The AI doesn't care. Ask for 20 ideas and you'll get 20 ideas. Some are silly. Some are terrible. Two or three are brilliant. Your job is to be the judge, not the generator.

## The volume rule

The biggest mistake is asking for 3 ideas.

Ask for 20.

Twenty is enough that the AI has to go beyond the obvious. It has to dig into variants, opposites, and weird angles. The first 5 will be predictable. Ideas 6-12 get interesting. Ideas 13-20 are sometimes the gold.

## A reliable brainstorming prompt

> You are a creative director who loves generating lots of ideas before narrowing down. I need to brainstorm [what you need].
>
> Context:
> - About me/my business: [brief]
> - Audience: [who this is for]
> - Constraints: [budget, time, tone, what to avoid]
>
> Give me 20 ideas. Don't filter. Include at least:
> - 3 obvious ideas (to cover the basics)
> - 3 unconventional ideas (weirder than I'd normally go)
> - 3 ideas someone in a totally different industry would do
> - 3 cheap-and-fast ideas
> - 3 ambitious "if budget was no object" ideas
> - The rest, whatever else you've got
>
> After the list, flag your 3 favourites and say why.

The structured request forces variety. Without it, you'll get 20 versions of the same safe idea.

## Real brainstorming examples

### Content ideas for the month

*"I need to brainstorm blog and social post ideas for my pet grooming salon's December content."* Add the template above. You'll get a month's content planned in five minutes.

### Names for a new product or service

*"I'm launching a premium dog grooming package for long-haired breeds. Need 20 name options."* Add the template. You'll have some real candidates.

### Ways to solve a problem

*"Our last 3 clients have complained about booking friction. Give me 20 ways to reduce it."* The AI's terrible ideas help you realise what you actually care about.

## What to do with the list

Three steps.

1. **Bin 15 immediately.** Trust your gut. If it makes you roll your eyes, skip it.
2. **Pick your 3 favourites.** Don't agonise. Pick fast.
3. **Ask the AI to help you plan just one.** *"Take idea 7. Give me a 3-step plan to do it this week."*

## One more trick

Brainstormed with the AI and still nothing feels right? Try this:

> None of these are quite it. What do they all have in common that I'm unconsciously looking for, and what's one idea that breaks that pattern?

AI is surprisingly good at this kind of meta-reflection. You'll often realise what you actually want.
