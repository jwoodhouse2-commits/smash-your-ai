Welcome. If you've ever opened ChatGPT, typed something in, got a reply that was *almost* right, and thought "there's something in this, but I can't quite make it work for me", then this course is for you.

We built it for two kinds of people.

- **Individuals** who want AI to save them time on real things. Emails, planning, writing, ideas.
- **Small business owners** who know everyone is talking about AI but haven't figured out how to actually use it in their business.

## How the course is structured

The course has three tiers, and you can work through them at your own pace.

1. **Beginner: AI Fundamentals.** What AI is, what the main tools are, and how to get going.
2. **Intermediate: Get Serious with AI.** Better prompts, custom tools, simple automations.
3. **Advanced: Claude Code and Agentic AI.** The cutting-edge stuff. Real AI in your workflow, not just chatting with it.

## How to use each lesson

Each lesson is designed to be short. Five to fifteen minutes. You can:

- **Tick lessons off** as you complete them. Your progress saves automatically.
- **Skip around** using the sidebar if you already know a topic.
- **Come back anytime.** If you're logged in, your progress follows you across devices.

## A promise

No jargon without a plain-English translation. No 45-minute videos when a 3-minute read will do. No "just trust me". We'll show you what AI actually is under the bonnet, so when it gets things wrong (and it will), you'll know *why*.
