If your AI answers keep coming out generic, off-tone, or just a bit meh, it's almost never the AI. It's the prompt. Here are the five mistakes that cause 90% of it.

## Mistake 1: Being too polite

> "Hi ChatGPT, hope you're well. I was wondering if you could maybe help me write an email to a client?"

You don't need to warm up. The AI doesn't care about pleasantries and pleasantries eat tokens. Start with the task.

**Fix:** Open with *"Write…"*, *"Draft…"*, *"Create…"*, *"Analyse…"*. Get to the point.

## Mistake 2: Leaving out the context

> "Write me a post about our new service."

What service? For who? What tone? On which platform? The AI has none of this, so it invents plausible defaults. The defaults are always generic.

**Fix:** Tell it **who you are**, **who it's for**, and **the tone you want**. Two sentences of context transforms the answer.

## Mistake 3: Asking for one thing when you want options

> "Write me an Instagram caption for this photo."

You'll get a caption. You'll have no idea if it's the best caption you could have got.

**Fix:** Ask for options. *"Write 5 captions, each in a different tone (warm, witty, professional, cheeky, direct). Pick your favourite and say why."* Now you've got material to choose from and a starting point for edits.

## Mistake 4: Accepting the first answer

The first AI answer is almost never the best one. It's a starting draft. Most people either use it as is (because it sounds okay) or give up on AI (because it's not quite right).

**Fix:** Talk back. *"This is close, but make it shorter and more conversational."* *"Good structure, but the tone is too formal. Redo it like you're texting a friend."* Iterate. AI is a conversation, not a vending machine.

## Mistake 5: Treating it like a search engine

> "What's the best CRM for a small business?"

The AI will give you an opinion with no context. Because it doesn't know yours.

**Fix:** Treat it like an assistant. Tell it about your business, your budget, your team size, what you've already tried, and what matters to you. Then ask the question. *"My business is a small graphic design studio, 3 people, £500/month budget, priority is ease of use over features. Given this, recommend 3 CRMs and say what each is best for."*

## The meta-fix

If you notice yourself reading an AI answer and thinking "this is a bit rubbish", stop. Don't blame the AI. Ask yourself: *"What did I leave out of my prompt that would have led to a better answer?"* Add it. Try again.
