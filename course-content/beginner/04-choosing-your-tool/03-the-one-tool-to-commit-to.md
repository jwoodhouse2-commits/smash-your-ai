A common pattern we see: someone signs up for ChatGPT, then hears Gemini is better, then tries Claude, then panics about missing the next thing, then ends up using none of them properly.

If that's you, this lesson is the antidote.

## The recommendation

Pick **one** AI and commit to it for 30 days.

Not because it's objectively the best. Because the compound benefits of using one tool consistently (learning its quirks, building prompt templates, getting fast at the UI) are bigger than any gap between the tools themselves.

## Which one?

Our default recommendation for most small business owners in 2026:

### Start with ChatGPT

Free tier is enough for the first month. Three reasons.

1. **The biggest community.** Every tutorial, prompt library, and YouTube video on the internet uses ChatGPT examples first. You'll learn faster.
2. **Voice mode is excellent.** This is the thing that will genuinely change your life if you commute, walk the dog, or think out loud.
3. **Custom GPTs.** Once you graduate to the [Intermediate tier](/course/intermediate/custom-gpts/what-a-custom-gpt-is), you'll see why this ecosystem matters.

### When to pick differently

- **You live inside Google Workspace.** Gemini is right there in Gmail and Docs. Lean into it instead.
- **You're a writer.** Claude genuinely is nicer to write with. Its drafts need less editing.
- **You're on a Microsoft-heavy team.** Copilot in Office will save you the most time for the least friction.

No wrong answer. **Just pick one and actually use it.**

## Your 30-day plan

Copy this into your diary.

- **Week 1**: Use it for every email where you'd normally sit and think. Use voice mode on one walk.
- **Week 2**: Run the "research a topic" prompt on something you'd normally Google. Save the prompt.
- **Week 3**: Draft three social posts, a meeting agenda, and one difficult email. Save the ones that work.
- **Week 4**: Summarise a long document or meeting transcript. Try a brainstorm for something at work.

By the end of 30 days you'll have:

- Built 5-10 prompt templates that actually save you time.
- Got fluent with the voice and file features.
- Have a real sense of whether this tool is the right one for you, or whether you want to try another.
