Modern AI chat is not just a text box any more. Each big tool now has voice, image, and file features built in. This lesson is a quick tour of what's actually useful and which tool to reach for.

## Voice mode

What it is: you talk, the AI talks back. Like a phone call with a patient colleague.

Useful for:

- Brainstorming while walking or driving.
- Practising for a presentation or difficult conversation.
- Learning something (it's a patient tutor).
- Quick questions when typing is annoying (cooking, DIY).

**Best tool:** **ChatGPT's voice mode** is the pick. Natural, fast, handles interruptions well. The paid version is notably better than the free.

## Image input

What it is: you give the AI a photo, and it describes, analyses, or answers questions about it.

Useful for:

- Photographing a receipt and asking *"add these to my expenses spreadsheet"*.
- Taking a picture of a form and asking *"what am I being asked for here?"*.
- Uploading a screenshot of a chart and asking *"what's the story here?"*.
- Identifying things: plants, mushrooms, symbols, clothing.

**Best tool:** all three (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) handle images well. **Gemini** is often fastest. **Claude** gives the most thoughtful analysis. Pick by taste.

## Image generation

What it is: you describe an image, and the AI makes one.

Useful for:

- Social media graphics.
- Blog post headers.
- Rough mockups and concepts.
- Anything where "close enough" beats "expensive".

**Best tool:** **ChatGPT** has image generation baked in (via DALL-E). **Gemini** also generates images, often with better realism. **Claude** does not generate images.

**Watch out:** AI-generated text (on signs, labels, packaging) is still unreliable in 2026. Check every word.

## File uploads

What it is: drop in a PDF, Word doc, spreadsheet, or image, and ask about it.

Useful for:

- Summarising long documents (covered in the last module).
- Extracting action items from meeting notes.
- Asking *"what's wrong with this?"* about a spreadsheet.
- Turning a scanned PDF into a plain-text version.

**Best tool:** **Claude** handles long documents the best (its working memory is huge). **Gemini** handles PDFs well if you've got a Google account. **ChatGPT** works fine too, but with tighter file size limits on the free tier.

## Web browsing

What it is: the AI can look things up online in real time.

Useful for:

- Researching recent news or trends.
- Fact-checking its own answers.
- Finding current prices, reviews, or specifications.

**Best tool:** **Gemini** has Google Search baked in by default. **ChatGPT** has browsing on the paid tier. **Claude** doesn't browse, but can read web pages you link to.

## Quick decision table

| Job | Reach for |
|---|---|
| Quick question while making dinner | ChatGPT voice |
| What does this receipt say? | Gemini or ChatGPT with image |
| Generate a rough social image | ChatGPT or Gemini |
| Summarise this 60-page PDF | Claude |
| Is this price actually good today? | Gemini |
| Thoughtful feedback on a document | Claude |
