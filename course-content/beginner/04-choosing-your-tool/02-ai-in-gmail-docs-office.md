You don't always need a separate chat tab to use AI. Google and Microsoft have both baked it into their everyday products. This lesson is the tour.

## Google Gemini in Gmail and Docs

If you use Gmail or Google Docs, Gemini is probably already there. Look for a little sparkle icon in the corner.

What it does well:

### In Gmail

- **"Help me write"** drafts a reply based on the context of the thread. Useful for replies you've written 500 times.
- **Summarise the thread** when you join a long email chain midway. Huge time saver.
- **Change tone**: take a draft reply, ask it to be more formal, more casual, or shorter.

### In Google Docs

- **"Help me write"** drafts the first version of a document based on a prompt.
- **"Tune" an existing selection**: shorter, more formal, more casual, etc.
- **Summarise** long docs directly inside the doc.

### In Google Sheets

Gemini in Sheets can write formulas from plain English. Try *"give me a formula that counts the rows where column B says 'paid' and column C is greater than 100"*. Solid time saver if you're formula-shy.

**Cost:** The basic Gemini features are included with most Google Workspace plans. Full-fat Gemini Advanced (the really clever version) is a paid add-on.

## Microsoft Copilot in Office

Microsoft's AI is called **Copilot**. It lives inside Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams.

What it does well:

### In Outlook

- **Summarise an email thread** at a glance.
- **Draft a reply** in your chosen tone.
- **Schedule a meeting** from an email conversation.

### In Word

- **"Draft with Copilot"** gives you a starter document from a prompt.
- **"Rewrite"** a selection in a different tone or length.
- **Summarise a long document** with a single click.

### In Excel

- **Analyse data** with plain-English questions like *"what's the trend in Q3?"*.
- **Highlight key numbers** it spots as unusual.
- **Generate formulas** from descriptions.

### In Teams

- **Summarise meetings** you joined late or missed entirely.
- **Catch up on a chat channel** you've been ignoring.

**Cost:** Copilot is a paid add-on (typically £22 per user per month). For most small businesses, this is the single biggest "AI in your workflow" decision to make.

## Which do you actually need?

Simple answer: **use whichever you're already in.**

- If your whole business runs on Google, use Gemini. It's free-ish and it's right there.
- If your whole business runs on Microsoft, Copilot is worth the £22. The Outlook and Teams features alone save serious time.
- Don't migrate to a new email suite just to get a different AI. Not worth it.

## A word of warning on auto-drafted emails

The "Help me write" and "Draft a reply" features are great, but they'll happily write a reply in about 30 seconds that sounds like **every other AI-drafted email** in 2026. Generic. Polite. Forgettable.

You still need to edit. The value is in not staring at a blank box, not in hitting send on the first draft.

## The takeaway

Two hours with Gemini in Gmail or Copilot in Outlook will save you a day a month on admin writing. That's the highest-ROI AI thing most small business owners can do.
