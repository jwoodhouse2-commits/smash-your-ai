Before we build one, let's be clear on what a custom GPT actually is.

## The plain definition

A custom GPT is a **saved setup** of ChatGPT that already knows:

- The role you want it to play.
- The rules it should follow.
- The voice it should write in.
- The files it can reference.
- (Optionally) The external tools it can use.

Instead of pasting your long prompt every time, you build it once and the GPT runs on those rules forever. You just type your actual task.

## The mental model

A regular ChatGPT conversation is like hiring a very capable freelancer you've never worked with before. Every time. You have to explain everything.

A custom GPT is like hiring a long-term assistant who already knows:

- Your business
- Your tone of voice
- Your preferences
- The templates and processes you use

You save hours every week because you stop re-explaining yourself.

## A real example

I run a small service business. I built a GPT called **"My Writing Assistant"**. Its instructions say:

- My business type, size, and audience.
- My tone rules (no jargon, short sentences, British English, no em dashes).
- My go-to structure for blog posts, emails, and social.
- Three examples of my own writing.

Every time I open it, I type something like:

*"Write me a blog intro about why most pet salons' websites don't convert."*

And out comes a draft that sounds like me. No preamble. No explaining.

That single GPT saves me about an hour a week. Multiply that by the number of repeatable tasks you do, and you're looking at significant time back.

## What custom GPTs are not

- **Not a separate AI.** Under the hood it's the same ChatGPT. Just pre-briefed.
- **Not magic.** If your instructions are weak, the output is weak.
- **Not just for technical people.** The interface is a plain-English form.
- **Not free.** You need a ChatGPT Plus (or equivalent) subscription to build them. You can **use** someone else's custom GPT on the free tier, but you can't build your own.

## What you can build

A few genres that work brilliantly.

### Writing assistants

Pre-loaded with your voice, examples, and content rules. Handles emails, posts, captions, newsletters.

### Specialist advisors

*"UK Small Business Tax Buddy"* loaded with the right government pages. *"GDPR Checker"* with the ICO guidelines.

### Process runners

*"Client Onboarding Assistant"* with your onboarding checklist, template emails, and standard questions.

### Creative tools

*"Business Name Generator"* with your brand values, example names, a checklist for what makes a good name.

### Teaching tools

*"Explain it like I'm 10"* with your rules for analogies and style.

## How custom GPTs fit in

Think of it as the graduation from **prompts** to **systems**.

- A prompt is good for one-off tasks.
- A saved prompt template is better for repeatable tasks.
- A custom GPT is best when the task has **rules, files, and context** the AI should always know.
