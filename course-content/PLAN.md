# Smash Your AI Course: Content Plan

Target depth: match [ccforeveryone.com/vibe-coding](https://ccforeveryone.com/vibe-coding/build-iterate). That means each lesson has:

- A realistic duration estimate (3 to 15 min)
- A short, focused point. Not a dumping ground for everything on the topic.
- At least one concrete "try this right now" exercise where it makes sense
- Plain-English, warm voice. Blog-article tone, not textbook.
- Occasional inline callouts linking to Starter Bundle resources (modal-triggered, they don't leave the course)
- A troubleshooting or FAQ section for anything with failure modes

## Voice rules

1. No em dashes, no en dashes. Full stops and commas do the work.
2. British English (colour, organise, realise).
3. Second person. "You" not "one".
4. Contractions are fine. It sounds like a person talking.
5. Short sentences. Mix in the odd one-word line for rhythm.
6. Concrete examples beat abstract principles every time. If you can't give an example, the lesson isn't ready.

## Target totals

- **Beginner tier:** 4 modules, 14 lessons, ~100 min.
- **Intermediate tier:** 4 modules, 16 lessons, ~130 min.
- **Advanced tier:** 4 modules, 14 lessons, ~130 min.
- **Grand total:** ~44 lessons, ~6 hours of reading plus hands-on time.

## Cross-cutting features to add during content work

1. **"Try this now" boxes** styled like the hero box on ccforeveryone. One per lesson where it makes sense.
2. **Starter Bundle callouts**, modal-triggered, that let buyers open the full bundle sales modal without leaving the lesson.
3. **Troubleshooting FAQ blocks** at the bottom of any lesson where something can go wrong (install errors, API quirks, GPT upload failures).
4. **Progress nudges**: if a user ticks 3 lessons in a row, a small celebration microcopy. If they haven't ticked anything in a module by the last lesson, a gentle "did you try these?" nudge.
