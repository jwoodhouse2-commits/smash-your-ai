Better AI prompting

Get dramatically better results from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini using proven prompting techniques.

Most people type a quick question into ChatGPT and wonder why the answer is so generic. We teach you meta-prompting, role-based prompting, and chain-of-thought techniques that professionals use to get specific, accurate, and useful responses every time.

You will learn how to structure prompts for different tasks, how to refine outputs through follow-up prompts, and how to get AI to produce content that sounds like you rather than a robot.

Custom GPTs and Gemini Gems

Build your own AI assistants tailored to your exact needs, no coding required.

Imagine having an AI assistant that already knows your business, your tone of voice, and exactly how you like things done. That is what custom GPTs and Gemini Gems give you. We show you how to create, configure, and deploy your own AI assistants for tasks like customer service, content creation, data analysis, and more.

No technical background needed. If you can fill in a form, you can build a custom GPT.

NotebookLM and AI research tools

Work with your own documents and data without worrying about AI making things up.

One of the biggest concerns people have with AI is hallucination, where it confidently gives you wrong information. Google's NotebookLM solves this by grounding its answers in your own documents. Upload your policies, reports, manuals, or research and ask questions that get accurate answers based on your actual data.

We cover how to set it up, what types of documents work best, and how to use it for everything from meeting prep to compliance checks.

Workflow automation with AI

Automate repetitive tasks and free up hours every week for the work that actually matters.

If you are doing the same task more than once a week, there is a good chance AI can do it for you. From auto-generating reports to processing emails to updating spreadsheets, we have automated all of this in our own businesses and saved thousands in the process.

Why we started Smash Your AI

Between us, we have spent over 40 years in education and have been at the forefront of AI adoption in our field. What started as experimenting with ChatGPT for lesson planning quickly turned into something much bigger. We automated report writing, student communications, data analysis, content creation, and dozens of other tasks that used to take hours every week.

The results were dramatic. We saved thousands of pounds in running costs, freed up countless hours, and built AI-powered tools that are now used by thousands of students. We even developed an AI tutoring platform through our education business, Smash Your Revision, which helps students who cannot afford traditional tutoring get quality support.

We realised that the skills we had developed were not just useful in education. Every business, from a local factory to a freelance designer, could benefit from what AI has to offer. The problem is that most people do not know where to start, or they have tried ChatGPT once and were not impressed. That is where we come in. We cut through the noise, skip the jargon, and teach you the practical skills that actually make a difference.

Our other businesses
